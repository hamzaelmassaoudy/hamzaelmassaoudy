# Hamza El Massaoudy

**Computer Science Researcher · Medical Imaging AI · Software Engineering**  
Nanjing, China

I work at the intersection of computer science, medical image analysis, and reproducible machine learning. My current research focuses on multimodal classification of primary liver tumors using multiphase CT, 3D deep learning, radiomics, clinical variables, and carefully validated fusion methods.

## Research focus

- Medical imaging and computer-aided diagnosis
- Multiphase CT and three-dimensional deep learning
- Multimodal fusion of imaging, radiomics, and clinical information
- External validation, domain shift, and model transportability
- Explainable and reproducible machine learning
- Statistical evaluation of clinical prediction models

## Current research direction

I am particularly interested in building medical AI systems whose performance is evaluated beyond a single internal dataset. This includes studying missing imaging phases, unavailable clinical covariates, preprocessing differences, feature imputation, model calibration, and external-cohort stress testing.

## Engineering background

Alongside research, I develop database-backed applications and web systems using Java, PHP, JavaScript, and relational databases. This software-engineering background supports my work on maintainable research pipelines, clear documentation, testing, and reproducible releases.
